# Mission Architect: Space Mission Design Game

A fun web game where students design, launch and fly a complete space mission.
Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, challenge: **Space Mission Design Game**.

**Play it:**(https://hi13-cyber.github.io/mission-architect/)

## How to play
1. Choose a mission: Climate Watch, Lunar Ice Hunter, Red Planet Relay or Storm Chaser.
2. Choose a difficulty and a team size (solo, or 2 to 4 players).
3. Go through the 5 steps: **Plan** (orbit, rocket, mission time), **Science** (instruments), **Build** (power, battery, computer, spare parts), **Comms** (antenna) and **Launch** (final check).
4. Watch the meters for budget, mass, power, battery, data and science. Cosmo, the astronaut guide, gives hints.
5. Press Launch. Watch the flight and read the score and the report to see why your mission succeeded or failed.

## What players learn
Every choice has a cost. A better instrument gives more science, but it adds weight, power use, money and data. Players learn the real trade-offs of space mission design.

## Features
- 4 missions and 4 difficulty levels
- Solo or team play for up to 4 players, with a different role in each step
- Guided mode with hints and a short dictionary
- Animated launch, random flight events and a score report
- Save, share and compare designs with design codes
- Best scores saved on your device

## NASA resources
We used the NASA resources on the challenge page as a guide: Design and Develop Science Missions, Mission Design Lab, NASA Mission Viz and GMAT.
The numbers in the game are simplified for learning. They are not real mission data.

## Tools
HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file. Font: Fredoka (Google Fonts). All graphics are drawn with code.
